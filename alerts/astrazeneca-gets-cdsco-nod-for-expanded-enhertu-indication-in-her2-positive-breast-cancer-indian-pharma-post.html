AstraZeneca's Enhertu Receives Expanded CDSCO Approval for HER2-Positive Breast Cancer in India

Published: 2026-07-28  |  Severity: High  |  Source: Original Publication

Article Analysis & Summary

Featured Article on Zero483. In a significant development for oncology patients in India, AstraZeneca has received an expanded indication approval from the Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO) for its groundbreaking therapy, Enhertu (trastuzumab deruxtecan), for the treatment of HER2-positive breast cancer. This regulatory nod marks a crucial step forward in providing more therapeutic options for patients battling this aggressive form of cancer, potentially extending hope and improving outcomes for a wider population.

Enhertu is an innovative antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) designed to precisely deliver chemotherapy directly to cancer cells that overexpress the HER2 protein, minimizing damage to healthy cells. This targeted approach has demonstrated remarkable efficacy in clinical trials, establishing Enhertu as a potent weapon against HER2-positive malignancies. The original approval of Enhertu by global regulatory bodies, including the CDSCO, marked a new era in cancer treatment, offering a lifeline to patients who had exhausted other therapeutic avenues.

The current expanded indication from CDSCO signifies that Enhertu's utility has been recognized for a broader patient population or in an earlier line of treatment for HER2-positive breast cancer within India. While the precise details of this expansion – such as specific patient populations, prior treatment requirements, or disease stages – are not immediately detailed in the announcement, an expanded indication typically allows physicians greater flexibility and opportunities to integrate this advanced therapy into their treatment strategies.

This approval is not just a regulatory milestone; it's a beacon of hope for thousands of Indian women diagnosed with HER2-positive breast cancer. It underscores the CDSCO's commitment to expediting access to cutting-edge therapies and reflects the ongoing efforts by pharmaceutical companies like AstraZeneca to address unmet medical needs globally. Patients and healthcare providers alike will now have more pathways to utilize a therapy known for its impressive clinical benefits, potentially transforming the treatment landscape for this challenging disease.

Context & Background

HER2-positive breast cancer accounts for approximately 15-20% of all breast cancer cases and is characterized by the overexpression of the human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2) protein. This overexpression drives aggressive tumor growth and progression, historically leading to poorer prognoses. The advent of HER2-targeted therapies, starting with trastuzumab (Herceptin) decades ago, revolutionized treatment by specifically blocking this growth-promoting pathway. However, many patients eventually develop resistance or progress, necessitating continuous innovation in the field.

Enhertu represents a significant leap forward as a next-generation antibody-drug conjugate (ADC). ADCs are often referred to as 'smart bombs' because they combine the specificity of an antibody (which targets a protein like HER2 on cancer cells) with the cell-killing power of a chemotherapy drug (the payload). Trastuzumab deruxtecan, the active ingredient in Enhertu, uses a cleavable linker that releases a potent topoisomerase I inhibitor directly inside the HER2-expressing cancer cells, leading to their destruction while sparing healthy tissue more effectively than traditional chemotherapy.

AstraZeneca, in collaboration with Daiichi Sankyo, has been at the forefront of developing Enhertu, investing heavily in research and clinical trials that have consistently demonstrated its superior efficacy in various settings of HER2-positive breast cancer, including those with low HER2 expression and gastric cancer. The regulatory approval process, particularly in a market as significant as India, involves rigorous evaluation of clinical data for safety and effectiveness. The CDSCO's decision to grant an expanded indication reflects confidence in Enhertu's clinical profile and its potential to significantly impact patient lives within the Indian healthcare system.
