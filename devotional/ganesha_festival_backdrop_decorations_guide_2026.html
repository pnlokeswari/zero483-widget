📋 4 Golden Rules for Choosing Your Backdrop

1. Match Backdrop Proportions with Idol Height:

Ensure your backdrop is 6 to 10 inches taller than your Ganesha idol to avoid crowding and create an elegant framing effect.

2. Tool-Free & Wall-Friendly Installation:

All 10 options feature self-standing bases, slot-in wooden stands, or damage-free peel hooks. No drilling into walls needed.

3. Lighting & Contrast Strategy:

For dark clay idols, opt for gold or sindoor-red backdrops. For light marble idols, a metallic circular arch with florals provides striking contrast.

4. Reusability Across the Festive Season:

Engineered wood, steel frames, and Pichwai hangings can be repurposed for Navratri, Diwali Laxmi Pooja, and Janmashtami.
