📋 4 Golden Rules for Choosing the Right Ganpati Backdrop

1. Match Backdrop Proportions with Idol Height:

Always ensure your backdrop is at least 6 to 10 inches taller than your Ganesha idol. A backdrop that is too short makes the setup look crowded, while the right height creates a royal framing effect.

2. Tool-Free & Wall-Friendly Installation:

All the options featured above feature self-standing bases or slot-in wooden stands. You never need to drill screws into living room walls or use damaging double-sided tapes.

3. Lighting & Contrast Strategy:

If your idol is dark clay or black stone, choose gold or sindoor-red backdrops (like Options #1, #3, or #4). For lighter marble or pastel idols, a metallic circular frame with floral greenery (Option #2) provides stunning visual contrast in photos.

4. Reusability Across the Festive Season:

Investing in sturdy engineered wood (MDF) or metal frames means you can repurpose the exact same backdrop for Navratri Durga Puja, Lakshmi Pooja on Diwali, and Janmashtami Jhula setups.
