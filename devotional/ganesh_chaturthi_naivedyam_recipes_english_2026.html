Rava Undrallu, Ukadiche Modak & Paal Thalikalu Recipes

Authentic step-by-step preparation of Lord Ganesha's three most beloved festive offerings

🕉️ The Significance of Ganesha Naivedyam

Offering homemade prasadam to Lord Ganesha is a cherished festive ritual during Ganesh Chaturthi. Tradition reveres offering 21 Modaks or a sacred platter of steamed delicacies made with love and purity. Here are the three most authentic, fail-safe recipes you can easily prepare at home.

🫓 Recipe 1: Steamed Rava Undrallu (Savory Kudumulu)

A classic steamed savory offering made with rice rava, tempered lentils, ginger, and green chilies

🧂 Ingredients

Rice Rava (or fine semolina)1 Cup
Water3 Cups
Green Chilies (finely chopped)2
Grated Ginger1 tsp
Chana Dal (Bengal Gram)1 tbsp
Mustard seeds, Cumin, Curry leaves, HingFor tempering
Pure Ghee or Sesame Oil2 tbsp
Grated Fresh Coconut & Coriander2 tbsp
SaltTo taste
1
🫕 Tempering
Heat ghee or oil in a pan. Splutter mustard seeds, cumin, chana dal, a pinch of hing, curry leaves, chopped green chilies, and ginger. Sauté until golden.
2
💧 Boil Water & Add Rava
Add 3 cups of water and salt. Bring to a rolling boil. Add grated coconut. Slowly pour the rice rava in a steady stream while whisking continuously to prevent lumps. Cover and cook on low heat for 5 minutes until water is absorbed and a smooth dough forms.
💡 Pour rava only when water is bubbling vigorously to avoid clump formation.
3
🙏 Shape & Steam
Allow the mixture to cool slightly. Grease your palms with ghee and roll into smooth, crack-free round balls (Undrallu). Place in an idli steamer plate and steam for 10 minutes. Serve hot with ghee.

🍡 Recipe 2: Traditional Ukadiche Modak (Steamed Sweet Modak)

Lord Ganesha's prime delicacy: velvety rice dough cups stuffed with aromatic coconut, jaggery & cardamom

🧂 Ingredients

Fine Rice Flour (Basmati or Idiyappam flour)2 Cups
Boiling Water2 Cups
Fresh Grated Coconut1.5 Cups
Crushed Jaggery1 Cup
Cardamom powder, Poppy seeds, Nutmeg1 tsp
Pure Ghee2 tsp
1
🥥 Prepare the Sweet Filling (Saran)
Melt ghee in a pan on medium flame. Sauté poppy seeds and grated coconut for 2 minutes. Add jaggery and stir until melted and integrated. Season with cardamom and nutmeg powder. Cook until moisture evaporates. Cool completely.
💡 Ensure the filling is not runny; overcooking can make the filling chewy when cooled.
2
🌾 Knead the Rice Dough (Ukad)
Boil water with a pinch of salt and a teaspoon of ghee. Turn off flame, add rice flour, mix vigorously, and cover with lid for 5 minutes. While still warm, knead into a silky, crack-free dough using moist hands.
⚠️ Always knead while warm; cold dough develops cracks when pleated.
3
🍡 Shape & Steam Modaks
Pinch a small dough portion, flatten with your thumbs into a thin cup with thin edges. Place a spoonful of coconut filling. Gently pinch 7 to 11 pleats around the circumference, bring the tips together, and seal at the peak. Steam in a steamer lined with banana leaf for 12-15 minutes.

🥛 Recipe 3: Authentic Paal Thalikalu (Sweet Milk Noodles)

Hand-rolled tender rice flour noodles simmered in rich creamy milk, sweetened with aromatic jaggery

🧂 Ingredients

Raw Rice Flour1 Cup
Full Cream Milk1 Liter
Organic Jaggery3/4 Cup
Ghee-roasted Cashews & Raisins2 tbsp
Cardamom powder & Saffron strands1/2 tsp
1
🌾 Make the Noodles (Thalikalu)
Knead rice flour with hot water and a pinch of salt into a soft dough. Roll small portions between your palms into 2-inch long thin tapered noodles, or press through a thick sev mould. Dust lightly with dry flour.
2
🥛 Cook in Simmering Milk
Bring milk to a gentle boil in a heavy pot and reduce slightly. Drop the rolled noodles gently into the milk. Do not stir immediately to avoid breaking them. Simmer for 8-10 minutes until the noodles float and turn tender.
3
🍯 Add Jaggery & Garnish
Melt jaggery separately in 1/4 cup water, strain, and let cool slightly. Turn off heat under the milk and slowly stir in the jaggery syrup. Garnish with saffron, cardamom powder, and roasted nuts.
⚠️ Crucial: Always switch off the flame before adding jaggery to avoid curdling the milk!

💡 Essential Prasad Offering Tips

1
Pure Devotion
Never taste prasad while cooking. Prepare it with a peaceful mind while chanting Lord Ganesha's sacred mantra: "Om Gam Ganapataye Namaha".
2
Sacred Number 21
Offer 21 Modaks or Kudumulu on a fresh banana leaf sprinkled with holy Ganga water and topped with a spoonful of pure cow's ghee.
