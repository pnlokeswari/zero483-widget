Simple Steps

Step-by-Step Pooja Procedure

Step 1

Preparing the Puja Space & Kalasham

Clean your puja area thoroughly. Place a clean wooden plank (peetha) and draw a simple rangoli or muggu. Spread raw rice on a banana leaf or plate, and place your silver or brass Kalasham on top. Fill the kalasham with clean water, a pinch of turmeric, kumkum, a coin, and cardamoms.

Step 2

Arranging Mango Leaves & Coconut

Arrange 5 or 9 fresh mango leaves around the rim of the kalasham. Apply turmeric and kumkum to a whole coconut and place it atop the leaves. Fold a new blouse piece neatly over the coconut to represent Goddess Varalakshmi.

Step 3

Preparing the 9-Knot Toram

Take a yellow thread (dyed with turmeric) and tie 9 knots, chanting Lakshmi's names with each knot. The 9 knots represent 9 forms of prosperity (Ashta Lakshmi + Varalakshmi). Place the toram near Goddess Lakshmi for worship.

Step 4

Vinayaka Worship & Avahanam

Begin the puja by worshipping Lord Ganesha (made of turmeric paste) to remove obstacles. Then, invoke Goddess Varalakshmi into the Kalasham with devotion using simple mantras.

Step 5

Offering Flowers, Naivedyam & Harati

Offer fresh flowers, akshata (turmeric rice), kumkum, and naivedyam (sweet payasam, vada, or fruits). Chant simple Lakshmi mantras, tie the Toram to your right wrist, and perform Harati with camphor.

🪔 Simple Mantras to Chant

1. Vinayaka Prayer (Beginning)
Om Gam Ganapataye Namah
2. Varalakshmi Avahanam (Inviting Lakshmi)
Om Shri Varalakshmyai Namah — Avahayami, Sthapayami, Pujayami
3. Main Lakshmi Mantra
Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I eat before performing Varalakshmi Vratham?

Yes! You may have milk or fresh fruits in the morning before performing the puja. Main meals are eaten after completing the puja and harati. Never fast against medical advice.

Can unmarried women or men participate?

Absolutely. Anyone in the family can participate in the prayers, offer flowers, and seek Goddess Lakshmi's blessings.

What should I do if I make a mistake during the puja?

Do not worry! Lakshmi values devotion over strict rituals. Fold your hands, ask for forgiveness (`Kshama Prarthana`), and continue peacefully.

What to do with the Kalasham items the next day?

Sprinkle the kalasham water around your house and plants. Use the coconut to prepare a sweet dish (prasadam) for your family. The rice can be cooked or donated.
