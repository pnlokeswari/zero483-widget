🔬 Key Ingredients Explained

🟡

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)

Reduces redness, strengthens the skin barrier, regulates sebum, and helps fade uneven skin tone. At a meaningful concentration, it significantly reduces the 5 signs of skin sensitivity: dryness, irritation, roughness, tightness, and weakened skin.

💛

Vitamin B5 (Panthenol)

A powerful humectant that draws moisture into the skin and locks it in. It accelerates barrier repair, soothes inflammation, and leaves skin feeling soft and supple even after cleansing.

💧

Hydrating Glycerin

One of the most well-researched skin moisturizers. Glycerin attracts water from the environment and deeper skin layers, providing immediate and sustained hydration that prevents post-wash dryness.

⚗️

Micellar Technology

Tiny micelle molecules surround oil and impurity particles and lift them off the skin gently — similar to how micellar water works, but in a rinse-off format. No scrubbing required, no barrier disruption.
