Pro Tips

Get the Best Results from Your Air Fryer

💧
Light Oil Spray Works Best
A light spray of oil (not drizzle) gives the crispiest results — especially for samosas and tikki.
🔄
Flip Halfway Through
Flip pakoras, chicken pieces, or fish halfway through for even golden crispiness on both sides.
📏
Don't Overcrowd the Basket
Air needs to circulate. Leave small gaps between pieces. This is exactly why the 8L is better for families.
🌡️
Pre-heat for 3 Minutes
A quick 3-min preheat at 180°C gives perfectly consistent results, especially for paneer and chicken.
